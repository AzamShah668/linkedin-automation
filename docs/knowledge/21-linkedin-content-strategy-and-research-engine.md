## 🔬 Content Research & Generation Algorithm

When a topic is passed into `DynamicPostGenerator.generate_full_linkedin_post_bundle(topic)`, the engine follows this 4-step workflow:

1. **Deep Research & Outlining**: Extracts exact technical specs, component files, and protocol names related to the topic.
2. **Copywriting Execution**: Drafts LinkedIn copy with high readability, short paragraphs, bold callouts, and structured bullet lists.
3. **Dynamic 4-Slide Renderer**: Calls `generate_4slide_carousel.py` to produce Slides 1, 2, 3, and 4 matching the topic title.
4. **JSON Package Bundle**: Saves ready-to-post package to `output/posts/packages/<post_id>_package.json`.
